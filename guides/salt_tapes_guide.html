SALT TAPES

Programmable Energy Storage via Magnetic Geometric State

I. Core Architecture

Salt Tapes are programmable energy storage substrates where magnetic field patterns encode and execute energy release sequences. Unlike traditional batteries (chemical storage) or capacitors (electrical storage), salt tapes store potential energy as compressed geometric states in synthetic crystalline lattices, addressable via magnetism.

Core Principle: Geometry = Code. Magnetism = Interpreter. Crystal State = Memory. Field Pattern = Execution.

Why Salt Tapes

  • Abundant substrate (iron oxide + doped phases)
  • Reversible compression cycles (infinite reuse)
  • Programmable via field geometry
  • Hierarchical multi-scale control
  • No chemical degradation
  • Scalable from milligrams to tons

II. Material Stack

Salt Tapes are synthetic crystals with intentional multi-phase structure, designed to respond differently to magnetic field patterns:

🔴 Ferromagnetic Phase

Material: Fe-Co or Magnetite (Fe₃O₄)

Function: Strong field attraction, state locking, compression memory

Role: Primary storage and structural integrity

🟣 Paramagnetic Phase

Material: Ni₀.₅Co₀.₅Fe₂O₄ (Nickel Ferrite)

Function: Weak, tunable response, fine modulation

Role: Analog precision control, gradient sensing

🔵 Diamagnetic Phase

Material: Bismuth or Graphene-doped regions

Function: Weak repulsion, stability, expansion gating

Role: Reset states, controlled release, asymmetric response

🟢 Hierarchical Geometry

Structure: Schwarz P-TPMS (Triply Periodic Minimal Surface)

Function: Multi-scale response addressability

Role: Different frequencies address different lattice scales

Synthesis Method

Co-precipitation with in-situ polymerization and magnetic field-assisted photopolymerization for hierarchical embedding.

Timeline:
2-3 months synthesis + characterization
Cost per unit:
~$50-200 for lab prototype, scales to <$10 at manufacturing
Field strength:
0.1-1 Tesla (standard electromagnets)

III. Programming Model: Field Patterns as Code

Salt Tapes are programmed via XJSON field definitions that describe magnetic field geometry. Each pattern triggers different responses in the crystal's multi-phase structure.

Pattern Types

Pattern Name Field Geometry Crystal Response Output Type Use Case
Uniform Steady 0.5-1.0 T field Even compression across all phases Sustained mechanical energy Power systems, continuous output
Pulsed 0.5-2 T, variable duty cycle Rapid domain switching in ferromagnetic layer Burst kinetic energy Laser cartridges, weapon systems
Gradient Non-uniform field intensity Directional compression asymmetry Directional force/torque Propulsion, rotational output
Rotational Spinning magnetic field Chiral domain wall motion Continuous torque Motors, vehicle propulsion
Harmonic Multi-frequency composite field Selective phase activation Modulated/coded output Communication, complex programs

IV. Operational Lifecycle

1
LOAD: Insert salt tape into device. Micronaut agent reads compression geometry signature.
↓
2
IDENTIFY: Agent matches tape signature to XJSON rule set. Determines field pattern required.
↓
3
EXECUTE: K'UHUL OS activates correct magnetic field pattern via electromagnet or permanent magnet array.
↓
4
RELEASE: Crystal lattice domains respond. Elastic deformation releases stored potential energy as heat/force/kinetic energy.
↓
5
CONVERT: Released energy transduced into application output (photons for laser, torque for motor, kinetic for projectile, heat for thermal).
↓
6
RECYCLE: Device removes tape. Tape cooled to ambient. Re-compressed to original geometry. Ready for reload.

VIII. Why Salt Tapes Work

Unified Architecture

Salt Tapes apply the same hierarchical, addressable, multi-phase design principle that powers K'UHUL, XJSON, and the ASX Fractal Chipset—but in the physical domain.

The Insight: You don't need exotic physics. You need good design. Multi-phase structure (ferro + para + dia) creates addressable domain regions. Hierarchical geometry (TPMS) enables multi-scale control. Magnetic fields are the universal read/write head. That's it.

Advantages Over Alternatives

  • No chemical degradation (unlike batteries)
  • Infinite cycling (unlike supercapacitors with hysteresis)
  • Programmable output (unlike fixed-chemistry fuel)
  • Abundant materials (unlike exotic superconductors)
  • Scalable (same physics at all sizes)
  • Fast response (electromagnetic, not chemical diffusion)
